Black Moon

Science Doesn't Explain Everything.

Titans aren't the only extraordinary things in this world. Some secrets were never created in a laboratory. Some have been here far longer than anyone realizes.

Series in Development
Same World. Darker Truths.

It Has Been Here the Entire Time

Black Moon is not another universe. It is the supernatural side of the Project Titan Universe—the same contemporary world seen through a different set of shadows.

Vampires. Werewolves. Demons. Magic. Things that people dismiss as myth, superstition, or stories told to explain what they do not understand.

Not everything extraordinary was created by Project Titan. Not everything can be explained by science.

Black Moon follows the people who live in that hidden world and the consequences that come when what has stayed concealed begins to move closer to the surface.

Inside Black Moon

The World Behind the Shadows

Black Moon opens a side of the universe most people never see. The supernatural has its own dangers, traditions, and ways of surviving—hidden beside ordinary life.

01 • Creatures

Things That Shouldn't Exist

Vampires, werewolves, demons, and other beings move through the same world as everyone else. Some hide in plain sight. Others survive because most people refuse to believe they exist.

02 • Magic

Power With Consequences

Magic is real, carried through knowledge and traditions kept outside ordinary sight. It has rules, risks, and consequences—and knowing how to use it does not make anyone safe from it.

03 • The Hidden World

Secrecy Is Survival

The supernatural survives because it stays hidden. Families, hunters, institutions, predators, and ordinary people cross paths while the wider world remains unaware of what exists just beyond what it can explain.
