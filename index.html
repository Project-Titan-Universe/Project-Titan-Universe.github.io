One Shared World

Every Story Leaves a Mark

Project Titan is a shared universe of extraordinary abilities, hidden histories, human choices, and things that have existed in the shadows far longer than anyone realizes.

Each series follows its own characters, conflicts, and corner of the world. Some stories begin with science. Others reach into something much older.

They can stand on their own, but they do not exist in isolation. People cross paths. Decisions leave consequences. Events in one story can reshape another.

Different stories. Different lives. One world.

About the Author

Justin Nead

Justin Nead is the creator and author of the Project Titan Universe, a growing shared world built around extraordinary people, hidden histories, difficult choices, and stories whose consequences reach far beyond a single book.

Project Titan brings together superhero fiction, grounded science fiction, thriller elements, and the supernatural within one connected universe.

SHIFT and IMOGEN are available now, with more stories from the Project Titan Universe on the way.
