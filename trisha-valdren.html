When Everything Changes

Trisha is nineteen and still figuring out what adulthood is supposed to look like. Work, friends, relationships, and the small routines of everyday life give her something familiar to hold onto.

ALTERED begins when that familiar life is disrupted and Trisha is forced into circumstances she never expected to face. Her story is not about becoming extraordinary overnight. It begins with what happens when an ordinary life changes without asking permission.
