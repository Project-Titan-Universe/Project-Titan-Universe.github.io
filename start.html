What Is Project Titan?

Project Titan is a connected universe of extraordinary abilities, hidden histories, buried experiments, human choices, and consequences that keep moving long after a chapter ends.

The powers matter. The battles matter. But the heart of the universe is the people living through them—and the choices they make when the world changes around them.

You do not need a guidebook before you begin. Start with Carter in SHIFT. The universe will open from there.
