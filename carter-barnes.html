Character Profile

The Beginning of Project Titan

Carter enters the Project Titan Universe at the point where the impossible becomes personal. His ability to manipulate his own density forces him to rethink what he believed about himself, his family, and the world around him.

SHIFT follows Carter as those questions expand into something much larger: extraordinary people, buried history, and consequences that reach well beyond a single life.
