Character Profile

When the Shadows Become Personal

Sarah has lived close to the consequences of Project Titan without fully belonging to that hidden world. As those consequences move closer, the distance between Sarah and Project Titan begins to disappear.

IMOGEN moves Sarah from the edge of that world toward its center, exploring what happens when the extraordinary is no longer something happening around her—but something that has become part of her own life.
