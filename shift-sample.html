Excerpt One

The confrontation

Carter has spent years refusing to fight back. Issac decides that restraint is weakness.

I closed the door behind me and turned around. Of course, my luck was out that day. Standing across from me, with a smile and his thick arms crossed over his barrel chest, Issac Winston. High school bully extraordinaire, star football player, and general Mr. Popular. Not my best friend, to say the least.

Popping his neck, Issac chuckled, “And here I thought my day was going to be uneventful.”

I stepped to walk around him, but he blocked my path. “Where you think you’re going, loser?”

Again, I tried to step around, “Just let me by, Issac. I’m not in the mood to deal with you right now.”

The minute the words left my mouth, and Issac’s grin turned to a glare, I knew it was a mistake. His hand snapped out for my shoulder, but I shoved it out with my forearm. His other fist came around and connected solidly with my jaw.

Excerpt Two

The impossible moment

The punch should have connected. The tackle should have stopped at Carter. Neither does.

Time slowed as I watched the boiling blood fill Issac’s face. Then his fist came at me, and I moved my hands to cover my face, closing my eyes. I heard the rush of his fist as it went past me, and he stumbled by as well. I lowered my arms and turned as he gained his balance and turned back to face me.

The look on his face was shock, but his voice, pure hatred, “How did you do that?”

I’m sure I looked at him like he’d lost his mind. “I didn’t do anything.”

He growled and charged at me. One other thing I learned, was how to brace for a tackle. Issac rushed forward at full speed, or at least as close as you can get to full speed in a bathroom. I watched as he barreled into me and then through me. A tingling sensation filled me where he ran through me.

Excerpt Three

Fear becomes a plan

Carter wants an explanation that belongs in the real world. His best friend sees a mystery they can solve.

Miles watched me for a moment, his pimply face all serious, except for the smirk playing at the edge of his mouth. Finally, he laid back on his cot and spoke with a lighthearted tone, “So, Issac just ran right through you, huh?”

Putting my head in my hands, “I... I don’t know... That’s what it looked like to me...”

Putting his hand behind his head, he stared up at the ceiling. “Interesting.”

I glared at him. “Interesting!? What do you mean interesting?”

Miles kept staring at the ceiling. “Interesting. Issac passed right through you, and you brushed my hand and broke three bones in my hand.” He turned his head with a knowing grin. “Look, we can figure this out. Just keep acting like it never happened, and I’ll come up with a plan to figure out what’s going on.”
